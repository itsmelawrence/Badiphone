# Badiphone landing page concept

Local, responsive homepage design for review. Open `index.html` in a browser or serve this folder with a static web server.

## Design direction

- Orange, charcoal and white are used as the client’s core palette.
- The repair workbench photograph is a **generated concept visual**, not a photograph of Badiphone’s actual workshop.
- The page gives separate routes to repairs, the future shop and device buyback. It presents the in-person workshop journey in Hellemmes and states that buyback estimates require physical inspection.
- Motion is limited to the initial hero and direct control feedback; reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Prototype boundary

The navigation, buyback category selector, FAQ and quote draft work locally. The quote draft creates a copyable summary and transmits nothing. Shop, booking, buyback pricing and live form handling need their production systems and client-approved content. No analytics, Search Console or payment integration is included in this design concept.

## Image generation

The hero image at `assets/repair-workbench.jpg` was generated with the built-in image tool and compressed for this page. Final prompt: “Premium editorial photograph of a technician’s hands carefully repairing a smartphone on a modern electronics workbench; credible circuit board and precision tools; charcoal surfaces with warm orange task lighting; wide horizontal composition with calm dark negative space on the left; no logo, words, watermark, signage, faces or home-visit context.”
